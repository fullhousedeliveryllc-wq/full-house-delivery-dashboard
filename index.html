    📅 Import Schedule Sheet

    📊

    Click to upload your schedule spreadsheet

    Excel (.xlsx) — in Google Sheets use File → Download → Microsoft Excel (.xlsx)

    How it works: every colored block on the weekly calendar becomes one entry — date from the column header, plus price, phone and email pulled out of the text, and the block's exact wording copied into the note. The cell color decides the category; you map each color to one of your categories in the preview (saved for next time), and anything mentioning a move is categorized as a move regardless of color. Light blue and orange both mean Furniture Store Warehouse (Miami Gardens and Tamarac), red means Furniture Store Floor Models and lime green Junk Removal; any other color imports as Uncategorized and is highlighted amber in the Entry Log so you can set it by hand. Blocks already imported are flagged as duplicates so re-uploading the same sheet is safe. CSV loses the colors — the .xlsx download is required.

        📄 Upload Bank Statement

        📋

        Click to upload or drag & drop your bank statement

        Supports CSV (.csv) and PDF (.pdf) files

        How it works: Download your bank statement as a CSV or PDF file from your bank's website. Upload it here and the system will automatically categorize transactions into your revenue and expense types based on the description. You'll get a preview to review and adjust categories before importing. Every line is imported — money out as an expense, money in as revenue — and anything the system can't confidently label comes in as Uncategorized (highlighted amber) so you can fix it later from the Finance Log. On a checking statement every deposit and Zelle/Venmo payment in is revenue and every payment out is an expense; on a credit-card statement every charge is an expense. Credit-card payments are skipped on both statements — the checking debit and the card's "payment thank you" credit — since that spending is already itemized as card charges and would otherwise be counted twice. CSV works best; PDF support extracts text-based data from common statement formats.
